# Home (start screen)

Scope: `/`, the opening of a live academic demo. Visitor mode: Persuade (the professor must understand the idea and believe the rigour within one viewport; the developer must be able to launch any showcase report instantly).

Audience and job: professor watching a projector; developer driving. Action: search a ticker or pick a showcase company. Proof: real engine snapshots and real validation numbers only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The engine's idea, made visible: a stock has many futures, so the product answers with a range. The hero's light is literally simulated price paths fanning out of the search field. Refuses the fintech split hero with a phone mock, logo strip and same-size feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Bright cool-white ground (#f6f8fb), deep navy ink (#0b1b33), a luminous fan of paths in the data-series colours (blue #3987e5, aqua #19b884, orange #ff6a3d) over a soft blurred glow of the same hues; dark navy product windows (#0b1220) floating on the light page holding real product UI. Geist display 700, tracking -0.04em; Geist Mono only for numbers. Slanted section edges.

STORY: See that the engine thinks in ranges (hero), feel it by touching the 3D lab, watch one real number get explained, read the honest evidence (including the failed ML model), then open a report.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed. Left: navy headline at display scale, one-sentence lead, a large search field with showcase chips beneath. From the search field's right edge, the fan of simulated paths spreads across the right two thirds to the viewport edge, its 10th to 90th percentile band labelled at the right; hovering a showcase chip reshapes the fan to that company's real volatility. Top nav minimal: brand left, page links right.

FORM: Data-palette fan hero plus product windows (Stripe-inspired storytelling, user-pinned; no seed roll: pinned direction).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
